CV for UK Jobs from Sri Lanka — British CV Format Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

The UK is an increasingly popular destination for Sri Lankan professionals, particularly given the Skilled Worker visa route. Here's how to format your CV to British professional standards.

UK CV Format — Key Differences from Sri Lankan CVs

UK CVs follow strict conventions that differ from Sri Lankan norms: No photo (illegal to use in hiring decisions in the UK), no date of birth, no nationality, no marital status, and no reference to religion. Maximum 2 pages for most roles (academic and medical CVs are longer). Clean, professional formatting with clear section headings. Reverse chronological work history.

UK CV Structure

Contact Details → Personal Statement (3-4 lines, tailored to each role) → Work Experience (most recent first, achievement-focused bullet points) → Education (most recent first) → Skills → Interests (optional, brief). The personal statement at the top is the UK equivalent of a professional summary — it should directly address the job description you're applying to.

Skilled Worker Visa Information

Many UK employers can sponsor Skilled Worker visas for positions in engineering, IT, healthcare, education, and skilled trades. When applying from Sri Lanka, include a brief note in your cover letter acknowledging the visa requirement and confirming your willingness to go through the process. Employers appreciate the transparency.

Where to Find UK Jobs from Sri Lanka

LinkedIn, Reed.co.uk, TotalJobs, Indeed UK, and sector-specific job boards (NHS Jobs for healthcare, CW Jobs for IT) are the main UK job platforms. The UK's National Health Service actively recruits internationally, including from Sri Lanka, for nursing and healthcare roles.
