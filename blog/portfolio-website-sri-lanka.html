How to Create a Portfolio Website in Sri Lanka — Career Booster Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

In Sri Lanka's increasingly digital professional landscape, a portfolio website is one of the most powerful career assets you can build. Here's how to create one that actually works.

Who Needs a Portfolio Website in Sri Lanka

Portfolio websites are essential for: designers and creative professionals, software engineers and developers, photographers and videographers, marketing professionals, architects and interior designers, writers and content creators, and freelancers of all types. For these fields, a portfolio website is now expected by most serious Sri Lankan employers and clients.

Platform Options for Sri Lankan Professionals

Wix — Easiest to build, good templates, no coding required. Free plan available, paid plans from USD 13/month.
Squarespace — Beautiful templates, particularly good for creatives. Plans from USD 16/month.
WordPress + Elementor — Most flexible and SEO-friendly. Requires some technical knowledge. Hosting available from Sri Lankan providers like LKDomain, Cambrean.
GitHub Pages — Free, ideal for developers. Shows technical ability through the platform choice itself.

Essential Portfolio Sections

Every professional portfolio website should include: About (your professional story and what you do), Portfolio/Work Samples (your best 8-12 projects, not everything you've ever done), Services (if freelancing), Testimonials (client/employer quotes), Contact (simple form or email link), and CV Download. Keep it clean, fast, and mobile-optimised — over 70% of Sri Lankan users access websites on mobile.
