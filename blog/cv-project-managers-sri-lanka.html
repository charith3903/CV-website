CV for Project Managers in Sri Lanka — PMP, PRINCE2, and Agile

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Project management roles are in high demand across Sri Lanka's IT, construction, and professional services sectors. Here's how to write a CV that wins PM interviews.

Lead with Certifications and Methodology

For PM roles in Sri Lanka, your certifications carry significant weight: PMP, PRINCE2, Certified Scrum Master (CSM), or PMI-ACP. Place these prominently after your summary. If you don't have a formal certification but have managed significant projects, consider prioritizing your PMP application — it meaningfully increases interview success rates in Sri Lanka's corporate sector.

Define Projects by Size and Complexity

Sri Lankan project management employers want to know the scale of what you've managed. For each role, quantify: project budget (LKR or USD), team size, timeline, number of stakeholders, and delivery outcome. 'Delivered an ERP implementation project worth LKR 120M, on time and 8% under budget, coordinating 14 cross-functional stakeholders across 5 business units.'

Show Methodology Versatility

Sri Lanka's IT sector predominantly uses Agile/Scrum, while construction and infrastructure projects use traditional waterfall methodologies. Financial and government projects often use PRINCE2 frameworks. Demonstrate your ability to work across methodologies — or tailor your CV's emphasis to match the specific organization and sector you're targeting.
