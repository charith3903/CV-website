How to Handle Employment Gaps on Your Sri Lankan CV

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Employment gaps on Sri Lankan CVs are more common than ever — and more accepted than ever. Here's how to handle them professionally.

Don't Try to Hide Gaps

Attempting to disguise employment gaps by using vague date ranges or omitting months is a mistake. Sri Lankan HR departments verify employment history during reference checks, and discovered dishonesty is a disqualifying red flag. Transparent, professionally framed gaps are infinitely preferable to apparent deception.

Proactively Explain in Your Cover Letter

A brief, confident explanation in your cover letter removes any concern before it becomes a question: 'I took a planned career break from January 2024 to August 2024 to complete my ACCA qualification, which I achieved in July 2024. I am now ready to return to full-time employment with enhanced financial reporting capabilities.' Context eliminates concern.

Common Gap Reasons Sri Lankan Employers Accept

Sri Lankan employers are generally accepting of gaps caused by: professional qualification studies, caring for a family member (particularly parents or children), medical recovery, accompanying a spouse on an overseas posting, or deliberate upskilling. These gaps, when framed positively, can even demonstrate admirable qualities — commitment to learning, family responsibility, or personal courage.

What to Say in the Interview

Be brief, factual, and forward-looking: 'I took time off to [reason]. During that time, I [any productive activity]. I'm now ready to return and I'm excited about this opportunity specifically because [reason].' Prepare this answer thoroughly — the gap question will always come up, and a fluent, confident answer reassures interviewers completely.
