Best CV Writing Service in Sri Lanka — What to Look For

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

The CV writing industry in Sri Lanka has grown significantly over the past few years, and with that growth comes a wide range of service quality. Some services deliver truly career-changing documents, while others produce generic templates that blend into the pile. So how do you choose the right one?

1. Look for Collaboration, Not Just Templates

The biggest red flag in any CV writing service is a one-size-fits-all approach. Your career is unique — your CV should be too. The best CV writing services in Sri Lanka take the time to understand your background, goals, industry, and target companies before writing a single word. At The Elegant Grid, we build your CV together with you, ensuring every detail is accurate, personalized, and powerful.

2. ATS Optimization Is Non-Negotiable

Over 75% of CVs submitted to large Sri Lankan companies are filtered by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human even sees them. A great CV writing service must understand ATS algorithms, keyword optimization, and formatting rules that ensure your CV passes these systems. Fancy graphics, tables, and headers can actually cause ATS rejection — your writer needs to know this.

3. Check Their Portfolio and Testimonials

Any reputable CV writing service in Sri Lanka should be able to show you real samples of their work. Look for diversity in industries, clean formatting, strong action verbs, and quantified achievements. Check Google reviews, Facebook reviews, and testimonials on their website. At The Elegant Grid, our portfolio speaks for itself — visit our Our Work page to see the CVs we've crafted for real Sri Lankan professionals.

4. Unlimited Revisions and Satisfaction Guarantee

A confident CV writer stands behind their work. Look for services that offer unlimited revisions until you're 100% satisfied. Avoid services that charge per revision — this is a sign they're not fully committed to your success. We don't charge you a single rupee until you're completely happy with your CV.

5. Turnaround Time and Availability

Job opportunities don't wait. A good CV writing service in Sri Lanka should be responsive, available, and able to deliver within a reasonable timeframe. At The Elegant Grid, we're available 24/7 and work with urgent timelines when needed.
