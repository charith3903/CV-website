CV Writing Tips for Engineers in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 6 min read

Sri Lanka's engineering sector spans construction, manufacturing, energy, telecoms, and IT — and each sub-discipline has its own CV expectations. But there are universal principles that apply to all engineering CVs in Sri Lanka. Here's the complete guide.

Registration and Professional Membership First

Being a Chartered Engineer (C.Eng) registered with the Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL) is a major credential that should appear prominently on your CV — either after your name or in a dedicated certifications section near the top. IESL membership, along with MIESL or FIESL designations, signals professional standing to every engineering employer in Sri Lanka.

Technical Competencies Section

Create a dedicated section listing your technical competencies relevant to your engineering discipline. For civil engineers: AutoCAD, STAAD Pro, primavera, road design, structural analysis, quality control. For electrical engineers: ETAP, PLC programming, HV/LV systems, power systems analysis. For mechanical engineers: SolidWorks, ANSYS, maintenance management, reliability engineering.

Project Experience is Everything

Engineers in Sri Lanka are hired for their project track record. Create a detailed project experience section listing major projects you've contributed to — project name, client/employer, value (LKR or USD), your role, and key deliverables. "Civil Engineer, Colombo Port Expansion Project (USD 500M) — led structural inspection team for 3 wharf structures, ensuring zero defects on delivery."

Quantify Your Engineering Achievements

Numbers make engineering CVs come alive: cost savings achieved, efficiency improvements, project delivery times, team sizes managed, safety records, and quality metrics. "Reduced maintenance downtime by 23% through implementation of predictive maintenance programme" tells a story that "responsible for maintenance" simply cannot.

Keep it ATS-Compatible

Many Sri Lankan engineering firms now use ATS. Include the key technical terms from the job description in your CV. Don't use tables for your technical skills section — ATS systems often can't parse them correctly. Plain text formatting with clear headings works best.
