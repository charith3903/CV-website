How to Apply for Jobs in Sri Lanka — Step-by-Step Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Job applications in Sri Lanka are more nuanced than simply submitting a CV. Here's the process that consistently leads to callbacks.

Step 1: Identify the Right Roles

Before applying widely, define your target role clearly: job title, industry, company type (local conglomerate, MNC, startup), and expected salary range. Focused applications to matched roles always outperform a scattershot approach.

Step 2: Customize Your CV for Each Application

Read the job description carefully. Mirror the exact language used for key skills and requirements in your CV. ATS systems used by many Sri Lankan companies match CVs against job description keywords — customization significantly improves your pass rate.

Step 3: Write a Targeted Cover Letter

For every meaningful application in Sri Lanka, write a brief, targeted cover letter. Reference the specific company, the specific role, and why your specific background makes you the right choice. Generic cover letters are worse than no cover letter.

Step 4: Choose the Right Submission Method

Most Sri Lankan job applications are submitted via: (1) job portal (TopJobs, xpat.lk), (2) company career portal, or (3) email directly to HR. For email submissions, use a professional subject line: 'Application for Senior Accountant — [Your Name] — 8 Years Commercial Experience'

Step 5: Follow Up

After 5-7 business days without acknowledgement, a brief, professional follow-up email is entirely appropriate in Sri Lanka's professional culture. 'I submitted my application for [Role] on [Date] and would like to confirm receipt and restate my strong interest in the position.' It demonstrates initiative without being pushy.
