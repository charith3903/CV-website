LinkedIn Recommendations for Sri Lankan Professionals — How to Get Them

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

LinkedIn recommendations are the closest thing to a reference letter that lives on your public professional profile — and they're incredibly powerful for Sri Lankan professionals.

Why Recommendations Matter in Sri Lanka

Sri Lankan employers and recruiters who visit your LinkedIn profile are directly influenced by what others say about you. Profiles with 3+ recommendations appear significantly more credible and trustworthy. They also add unique, human language to your profile that boosts your profile's keyword diversity.

How to Ask for a LinkedIn Recommendation

Send a personalized message to the person you're requesting a recommendation from: 'Hi [Name], I'm actively updating my LinkedIn profile and would be incredibly grateful for a recommendation from you, based on our work together at [Company] on [Project/Role]. I'm particularly hoping you might mention [specific skill/achievement]. I'm happy to write one for you in return — just let me know.' Make it easy for them by being specific about what you'd like them to highlight.

Who to Ask in Sri Lanka's Professional Context

The most impactful recommendations come from: direct managers and supervisors, senior mentors in your field, clients you've served well, and respected professional colleagues. In Sri Lanka's relationship-focused business culture, a recommendation from a known industry leader carries extraordinary weight.

How to Write a Great Recommendation for Someone

Be specific, use numbers where possible, and describe the person's impact on actual work outcomes. 'Nalinda consistently delivered projects ahead of schedule while managing multiple stakeholders — his ability to lead complex cross-functional projects resulted in our division achieving its annual targets 6 weeks early' is far more powerful than 'He is a hardworking and dedicated professional.'
