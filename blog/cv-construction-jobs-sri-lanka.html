CV for Construction Jobs in Sri Lanka — Engineers and Managers

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Sri Lanka's construction sector is booming with major infrastructure projects, Port City Colombo, and private real estate development. Here's how to position yourself for the best opportunities.

Lead with Scale of Projects Delivered

Construction employers in Sri Lanka evaluate candidates primarily by the scale and complexity of projects they've managed. Lead your CV with project credentials: 'Civil engineer with 10 years of experience managing large-scale infrastructure projects in Sri Lanka. Led the structural engineering and site management for three high-rise developments valued at USD 180M total in Colombo's central business district.'

Professional Registration and Qualifications

For senior roles in Sri Lanka's construction sector, include: IESL (Institution of Engineers Sri Lanka) membership, Chartered Engineer status, ICTAD contractor registration grade (if applicable), and any RICS qualifications for QS/commercial roles. These professional registrations are often mandatory requirements for government contracts.

Technical and Safety Certifications

Include relevant technical certifications: AutoCAD/Revit proficiency, project management qualifications, Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) certifications, and ISO quality management system knowledge. Safety qualifications are particularly valued — Sri Lanka's construction sector is increasingly focused on site safety standards following several high-profile incidents.
