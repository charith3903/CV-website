CV for HR Professionals in Sri Lanka — CIPM and SHRM Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

HR professionals in Sri Lanka are increasingly expected to be strategic business partners, not just administrative support. Your CV must reflect this evolution.

Lead with Your HR Philosophy and Scope

Open with a professional summary that establishes your HR identity: 'Strategic HR leader with 12 years of experience driving organizational transformation across Sri Lanka's manufacturing and FMCG sectors. CIPM Fellow. Expertise in talent acquisition, learning and development, and HR digitisation. Track record of reducing attrition by 40% and improving employee engagement scores at 3 of Sri Lanka's largest manufacturing groups.'

Demonstrate Both Strategic and Operational Capability

The best HR CVs in Sri Lanka show breadth: strategic (HR policy development, organisational design, culture building, C-suite advisory) alongside operational (payroll management, EPF/ETF compliance, labour law, disciplinary procedures). Balance both — purely operational HR CVs cap out at middle management, while purely strategic CVs lack credibility without operational foundation.

Quantify HR Impact

HR professionals often struggle to quantify their work — but numbers are possible. 'Reduced average time-to-fill from 67 days to 31 days through process redesign', 'Reduced attrition from 28% to 14% over 24 months', 'Designed and delivered L&D programme reaching 1,400 employees across 8 sites.' Find your numbers.
