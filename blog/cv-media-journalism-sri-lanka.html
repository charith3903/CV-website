CV for Media and Journalism Jobs in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Media and journalism CVs in Sri Lanka are unique — your portfolio and demonstrated work carries more weight than almost any other industry. Here's how to present both effectively.

Your Portfolio Is Your Primary Credential

For any media role in Sri Lanka, your portfolio of published work, broadcast clips, or digital content is your most important asset. Include a link to your portfolio website or a curated selection of your best work. For broadcast journalists and presenters, a showreel link is essential. For print and digital journalists, links to your top published pieces.

The Media CV Structure

Unlike corporate CVs, media CVs should be structured: Summary → Key Skills → Broadcast/Print/Digital Work Highlights → Employment History → Education → Technical Skills (cameras, NLE software, CMS platforms). List specific programmes you've presented, produced, or contributed to, with audience reach where known.

Language Skills Are Essential

Specify your language proficiencies clearly for Sri Lankan media roles: English, Sinhala, and Tamil fluency levels. Many Sri Lankan media organisations — particularly state media and national broadcasters — require bilingual or trilingual professionals. Private sector digital media increasingly requires English-primary content creators.
