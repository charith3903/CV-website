ATS-Optimized CV for Sri Lanka Jobs: The Complete Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 7 min read

Most Sri Lankan job seekers don't realize that their CV is being rejected by software — not humans — long before any recruiter ever sees it. Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are used by most large companies and recruitment agencies in Sri Lanka to filter applications automatically. Here's everything you need to know.

What is an ATS and How Does It Work?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software that scans incoming CVs for relevant keywords, qualifications, and formatting. It ranks candidates based on how well their CV matches the job description. CVs that don't meet the threshold are automatically rejected. Popular ATS platforms used in Sri Lanka include SAP SuccessFactors, Workday, and Greenhouse.

Why Most Sri Lankan CVs Fail ATS

The most common ATS failures include: using tables and text boxes (ATS can't read them), having an image-based CV or PDF with scanned content, using creative fonts and symbols, and missing job-specific keywords. Even a beautifully designed CV can score zero on ATS if it uses the wrong format.

How to Optimize Your CV for ATS

First, use standard section headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills — not creative alternatives like "My Journey" or "What I've Done." Second, match keywords directly from the job description. If the job posting says "project management," your CV must include exactly those words. Third, use a plain, single-column format. Avoid headers, footers, tables, and text boxes. Fourth, save your CV as a .docx or plain PDF (not a scanned PDF).

The Right Keywords for Sri Lankan Jobs

Research the keywords most used in your industry. For IT jobs in Sri Lanka: JavaScript, React, Agile, Scrum, AWS. For banking: KYC, AML, credit risk, financial modelling. For marketing: digital marketing, SEO, Google Analytics, brand strategy. Use these keywords naturally throughout your CV — not just stuffed in a list.

Test Your CV Before Applying

Use free tools like Jobscan or Resume Worded to test your CV's ATS compatibility before applying. Upload your CV and the job description — these tools will show you exactly how well your CV matches the role and what keywords you're missing.
