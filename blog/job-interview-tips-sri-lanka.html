Job Interview Tips for Sri Lanka — How to Impress Any Interviewer

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 7 min read

Sri Lankan job interviews have their own culture, etiquette, and expectations. Here's the comprehensive guide to performing at your best.

Research the Company Thoroughly

Sri Lankan interviewers are immediately impressed by candidates who have done their homework. Know the company's history, recent news, key products/services, competitive position, and strategic direction. Reference specific company information naturally during the interview to demonstrate genuine interest.

Prepare Your Stories Using STAR

For competency-based interviews — common in Sri Lanka's corporate sector — prepare 6-8 achievement stories using the STAR format: Situation (context), Task (your responsibility), Action (what you specifically did), Result (measurable outcome). Have stories for: leadership, problem-solving, conflict resolution, working under pressure, innovation, and customer/stakeholder management.

Dress Professionally

Appearance matters enormously in Sri Lanka's professional culture. For corporate interviews: men should wear a formal suit or at minimum collared shirt and formal trousers; women should wear formal business attire. Err on the side of being overdressed rather than underdressed — it's far safer in Sri Lanka's conservative corporate environment.

Arrive Early and Be Courteous to Everyone

Arrive at least 10-15 minutes early. Be pleasant and professional with the receptionist, security, and everyone you meet from the moment you enter — in Sri Lanka's relationship-oriented culture, these interactions are often noted and can influence hiring decisions.

Handle Salary Questions Strategically

If asked about salary expectations, provide a range based on market research: 'Based on my experience and the market for this role, I'm looking at LKR X to Y, though I'm open to discussing based on the full package.' Never provide a very specific number too early in the process.
