CV for Saudi Arabia Jobs from Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Saudi Arabia is one of the largest destinations for Sri Lankan expatriate workers at all skill levels. For professional roles, here's how to present yourself effectively.

Saudi CV Format Expectations

Saudi CVs typically follow Gulf standards: professional photo, personal details (nationality, date of birth, marital status, religion if relevant), and 2-3 pages for professional roles. Many large Saudi employers and government entities use application forms rather than accepting open-format CVs — always follow the specific application instructions.

Vision 2030 — Sectors in High Demand

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 transformation has created enormous professional demand in: tourism and hospitality (Neom, Red Sea Project), entertainment and events, healthcare (Saudi Health expansion), fintech and digital banking, construction and infrastructure, and professional services. Sri Lankan professionals with skills in these areas have significant opportunities.

Iqama and Sponsorship Process

Most Saudi professional employment requires an Iqama (residency permit) sponsored by your employer. This is a normal part of professional employment in Saudi Arabia. Recruitment is typically managed through Saudi-licensed employment agencies and direct employer recruitment. Many Sri Lankan recruitment agencies have established Saudi employer relationships — check SLBFE registered agencies for legitimate placements.
