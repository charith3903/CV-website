How Long Does Professional CV Writing Take in Sri Lanka?

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 3 min read

One of the most common questions about professional CV writing in Sri Lanka is: how long does it actually take? Here's what you should expect.

Standard CV Writing Timeline

A professional CV writing service in Sri Lanka typically takes 3-5 business days for a high-quality result. This includes: an initial consultation or information-gathering questionnaire (day 1), CV writing and structuring by the writer (days 2-3), first draft delivery (day 3-4), client review and feedback, and revision and final delivery (day 4-5). Rushing this process compromises quality — if a service promises a professional CV in 2 hours, manage your expectations.

Rush Service Options

Many professional CV services in Sri Lanka, including The Elegant Grid, offer rush delivery (within 24-48 hours) for an additional fee. This is appropriate if you have an urgent application deadline. However, standard timeline delivery almost always produces a superior result — if you can plan ahead, do so.

What Takes the Most Time

The most time-intensive part of professional CV writing is the content development — extracting, framing, and quantifying your achievements in ways that are both accurate and compelling. This is where the real value is created, and it cannot be rushed without compromising quality.
