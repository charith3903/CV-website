Portfolio Tips for Sri Lankan Designers — How to Showcase Your Work

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

In Sri Lanka's growing creative and digital economy, your portfolio is worth more than your degree and CV combined. Here's how to build one that wins.

Quality Over Quantity — Always

Sri Lankan design hiring managers and clients would rather see 8 exceptional pieces than 25 mediocre ones. Be ruthless about curation. Every piece in your portfolio should represent work you're genuinely proud of, work that shows a specific skill or sensibility, and work that's relevant to the type of projects you want to win.

Show Your Process, Not Just the Outcome

The best design portfolios in Sri Lanka show: the brief, your research and ideation, wireframes or sketches, iterations, and the final solution. This process documentation demonstrates design thinking — the ability to solve problems strategically, not just produce attractive visuals. This is what separates senior designers from junior ones in the Sri Lankan market.

Include Context and Results

For each project, explain: the client's objective, the design challenge you solved, your approach, and the result if measurable. 'Designed the brand identity for a Colombo-based fintech startup that raised LKR 120M in Series A funding within 6 months of launch.' Context transforms pretty pictures into evidence of professional capability.

Platforms for Sri Lankan Designers

Behance is the primary portfolio platform for Sri Lankan designers seeking both local and international opportunities. Dribbble is excellent for UI/UX work. Your own domain (firstname-lastname.com) with a custom portfolio site signals serious professionalism. Instagram is increasingly important for visual creatives targeting local clients.
