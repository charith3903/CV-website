CV for Overseas Jobs from Sri Lanka — International Career Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Working abroad is a significant aspiration for many Sri Lankan professionals. Here's how to adapt your CV to maximise your chances in international job markets.

Research the Target Country's CV Expectations

CV expectations vary significantly by country. The US and Canada use 'resumes' — typically 1 page for early career, 2 pages maximum. The UK uses 'CVs' — typically 2 pages, chronological. UAE and Gulf countries expect 2-3 page CVs often including a photo. Australia/NZ: 2-3 pages, no photo required. Germany: a Lebenslauf format with photo is expected. Research your target country's norms before applying.

Highlight International Competencies

Overseas employers hiring Sri Lankan professionals often look for: strong English communication skills (demonstrated, not just stated), cross-cultural awareness, international project experience, and globally recognized qualifications. Emphasize these throughout your CV — they directly address concerns international employers may have about hiring from Sri Lanka.

Address the Relocation Factor Directly

Many overseas employers hesitate about relocation costs and commitment. If you're willing to relocate at your own expense, state it clearly in your cover letter. Include a line in your CV summary: 'Sri Lankan national. Eligible for [country] work visa. Willing to relocate immediately.' This removes uncertainty and reassures employers.
