CV Tips for Sri Lankans Living and Working Abroad

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Sri Lankan professionals working abroad have unique CV advantages and challenges. Here's how to make the most of your international experience.

Position International Experience as an Asset

When applying back to Sri Lanka or to other international markets, your overseas experience is a significant differentiator. Many Sri Lankan employers actively seek professionals with international exposure — especially from the UAE, UK, Australia, or USA — as it signals global standards of professionalism and broader perspective.

Address the Perception of Being 'Overqualified'

Some Sri Lankan employers may assume that candidates returning from overseas are either seeking emergency employment or will leave again quickly. Address this proactively in your cover letter: 'I have decided to return to Sri Lanka permanently to build my long-term career here, and I am committed to contributing to [Company]'s growth with the international perspective and skills I've developed over the past [X] years.'

Translate Your Overseas Context for Sri Lankan Readers

When listing overseas employers, provide brief context: 'Emirates NBD (UAE's largest bank, USD 19B assets)' — because a Sri Lankan HR manager may not recognise the brand without context. Similarly, convert any salaries or metrics from foreign currencies to LKR or USD for clarity.
