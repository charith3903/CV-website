LinkedIn vs CV in Sri Lanka — Which Do Employers Check First?

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

In 2026's Sri Lankan job market, both your LinkedIn profile and your CV are essential — but they serve different purposes and are checked at different stages of the recruitment process.

The Typical Sri Lankan Hiring Process

In most Sri Lankan companies, the sequence goes like this: (1) Job advertisement posted, (2) CVs submitted via email or job portal, (3) HR screens CVs for basic qualification match, (4) Shortlisted candidates' LinkedIn profiles checked to verify information and assess cultural fit, (5) Interview invitations sent to those who pass both screens.

Your CV Gets You the Interview Consideration

Your CV is the primary document that determines whether you're initially considered. It's formatted for scanning, keyword-optimized for ATS, and designed to communicate your qualifications quickly and clearly. Your CV must be exceptional — because it's the first gate you need to pass.

Your LinkedIn Profile Seals the Deal

Once a Sri Lankan recruiter or HR manager is interested in your CV, they will Google your name and visit your LinkedIn profile to: verify your employment history, look for social proof (recommendations, endorsements), assess your professional presence and engagement, and check for any red flags. A weak or inconsistent LinkedIn profile at this stage can cost you the interview.

How to Make Both Work Together

Ensure consistency between your CV and LinkedIn — same dates, same job titles, same key achievements. Your LinkedIn can be richer and more expansive; your CV should be concise and targeted. Both should carry a consistent professional brand and narrative. Together, they create a compelling, multi-layered picture of who you are as a professional.
