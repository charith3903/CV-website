How to Write a CV for Government Jobs in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 6 min read

Government job applications in Sri Lanka are unlike private sector applications. They follow formal processes, require specific documentation, and expect a particular tone and format. Here's everything you need to know to prepare a winning government job CV for Sri Lanka.

Understanding Government Job Applications in Sri Lanka

Most government job vacancies in Sri Lanka are advertised by the Department of Examinations, Public Service Commission, or individual ministries. Applications are typically submitted with a formal bio-data form and sometimes a full CV. Understanding whether the role requires a bio-data form, a formal CV, or both is the first step.

The Formal Bio-Data Format

Sri Lankan government applications often require a bio-data that includes: Full name in block letters, NIC number, date of birth, permanent address, educational qualifications (GCE O/L, A/L, degree), professional qualifications, employment history in government departments, language proficiency (Sinhala, Tamil, English), and contact details. Fill in every section — blank fields look careless.

Language and Tone

Government CVs in Sri Lanka must use formal, polite language. Avoid casual or conversational phrasing. Use complete sentences where required. If applying for a bilingual position, you may need to submit your CV in both Sinhala and English — ensure translations are accurate and professionally done.

Emphasising Public Service Values

Government employers value reliability, integrity, teamwork, public service commitment, and adherence to procedure. Highlight experiences that demonstrate these qualities — whether from previous government roles, voluntary work, or community involvement. Mentions of Awards, commendations, or recognition in your career history are especially impactful.

Required Supporting Documents

Alongside your CV, government job applications in Sri Lanka typically require: certified copies of educational certificates, NIC copy, birth certificate, police clearance certificate (for security-sensitive roles), character reference letters, and passport-size photographs. Always check the specific requirements for each advertisement.

Common Mistakes in Government Applications

Submitting uncertified document copies, missing the deadline by even one day, or leaving required fields blank are all instant disqualifiers. Sri Lankan government application processes are strict about procedure — follow instructions precisely.
