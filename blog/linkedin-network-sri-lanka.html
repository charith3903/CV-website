How to Build Your LinkedIn Network in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

A strong LinkedIn network in Sri Lanka can open doors that no job application can. Here's how to build one that matters.

Start with Your Existing Network

Import your phone contacts and email address book into LinkedIn to find people you already know. Connect with university classmates, past colleagues, industrial training supervisors, lecturers, and family contacts who are working professionals. These warm connections are your foundation.

Connect with Industry Leaders in Sri Lanka

Identify the top 20-30 professionals in your field in Sri Lanka — CEOs, directors, thought leaders, and industry commentators. Follow their profiles and engage genuinely with their content before sending connection requests. A personalized request that references a post they shared will always get a higher acceptance rate.

Join Sri Lanka LinkedIn Groups

Search for and join active LinkedIn groups related to your industry in Sri Lanka: 'Sri Lanka Banking Professionals,' 'ICT Industry Sri Lanka,' 'Marketing Professionals Sri Lanka,' and similar communities. Active participation in these groups builds visibility within your target professional community.

Give to Get

The most effective networkers in Sri Lanka give before they ask. Endorse skills, write recommendations, share useful articles, comment on others' achievements, and celebrate your network's milestones. When you consistently add value to your network, reciprocation naturally follows.
