CV for Hotel & Tourism Jobs in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Sri Lanka's tourism and hospitality industry has rebounded strongly and is one of the country's largest employers. Top hotels like Shangri-La, Cinnamon Hotels, Jetwing, Aitken Spence, and John Keells Holdings are always hiring talented hospitality professionals. Here's how to write a CV that gets you there.

Lead with Customer Service Excellence

The hospitality industry in Sri Lanka — and globally — is built on service. Your CV must reflect a customer-first mindset from the very first line. Your professional summary should explicitly mention your passion for guest experience, your service philosophy, and your track record of positive guest interactions. Guest satisfaction scores, TripAdvisor ratings contributions, and repeat guest rates are gold for hospitality CVs.

International Brand Experience is a Major Asset

If you've worked for an internationally branded hotel (Hilton, Marriott, IHG, Hyatt), make sure this is prominently featured. International hotel groups in Sri Lanka and abroad highly value brand-standard training and experience. Include the brand, property name, star rating, and number of rooms for context.

Include Language Skills

In Sri Lankan hospitality, multilingualism is a major competitive advantage. Fluency in English is baseline. Additional proficiency in Mandarin, German, Japanese, French, or Arabic can significantly increase your employment and promotion prospects in Sri Lanka's international-focused resorts and hotels.

Hospitality-Specific Qualifications

Include any SLTDA (Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority) certifications, Vocational Training Authority (VTA) qualifications, SLITHM (Sri Lanka Institute of Tourism and Hotel Management) degrees, or international hospitality management qualifications (WSET, City & Guilds). These demonstrate commitment to the profession.

Showcase a Range of Operational Experience

List the departments you have experience in: Front Office, Food & Beverage, Housekeeping, Spa & Wellness, Kitchen, Sales & Marketing, Revenue Management, or Events. Cross-departmental experience shows versatility and is especially valued in smaller boutique properties.
