CV for Lawyers in Sri Lanka — Legal CV Writing Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Sri Lanka's legal sector is highly credential-focused. Here's how to present your legal qualifications and experience in the most compelling way.

Qualifications First

Legal CVs in Sri Lanka should prominently feature: your LL.B, Attorney-at-Law admission (with date of admission to the Bar), any postgraduate legal qualifications (LL.M, MCL), and Bar Association of Sri Lanka membership. International qualifications (Barrister-at-Law, LPC, NY Bar) should also be prominently placed if relevant.

Categorize Your Practice Areas

Clearly identify your practice areas: Commercial Law, Litigation, Intellectual Property, Banking and Finance, Labour Law, Constitutional Law, Corporate Law, or a combination. Be specific — 'Specialising in commercial litigation and intellectual property disputes for multinational clients in Sri Lanka' is far more useful to a hiring partner than 'General legal practice.'

Notable Cases and Transactions

Where confidentiality permits, reference significant cases you've worked on, transactions you've advised on, or briefs you've appeared for. Even describing them anonymously ('Advised a leading Sri Lankan bank on a LKR 2B securitisation transaction') adds enormous credibility to a legal CV.

For Law Firm Applications

Top Sri Lankan law firms (Julius and Creasy, Varners, FJ&G De Saram, Nithyanandam, Sudath Perera Associates) look for: academic excellence, internship and training history, publication record, and demonstrated client advisory experience. A strong academic record from a recognized Sri Lankan university or overseas institution remains highly influential in legal hiring.
