CV for NGO Jobs in Sri Lanka — Development Sector Careers

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Sri Lanka has a large and active development sector, with hundreds of national and international NGOs, UN agencies, and development finance institutions operating across the country. Here's how to position your CV for this unique job market.

Development Sector Language

NGO and INGO CVs in Sri Lanka use sector-specific language that differs from corporate CVs. Familiarise yourself with and use: 'Theory of Change', 'Results Framework', 'M&E' (Monitoring and Evaluation), 'Stakeholder Engagement', 'Community Mobilisation', 'Gender Mainstreaming', 'Capacity Building', 'Donor Reporting.' The right language signals insider familiarity with how the development sector operates.

Quantify Social Impact

Development sector employers are outcome-focused. Quantify your social impact: 'Reached 12,000 beneficiaries across 42 communities in the Northern Province', 'Managed USD 1.2M project budget with donor reporting to USAID, UNDP, and World Bank', 'Trained 340 community health workers across 3 districts.'

Donor Relationships and Grant Management

Experience with specific donors (USAID, EU, UKAID, UNICEF, UNDP, World Bank, ADB) is highly valued in Sri Lanka's development sector. If you have experience managing grants from these donors — including reporting, compliance, and relationship management — highlight it prominently. This experience is a key differentiator for senior NGO roles.
