CV for Your First Job in Sri Lanka — Complete Fresh Graduate Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Everyone starts somewhere. Here's how to write a compelling first-job CV in Sri Lanka even with no formal work experience.

Lead with Your Education

For a fresh graduate in Sri Lanka, your education is your primary credential. Place it prominently: Degree title, University, graduation date, and GPA if strong (above 3.0/above second class upper). Mention your major subjects, final year project, and any academic awards or distinctions. If you're from a respected Sri Lankan institution (University of Colombo, Moratuwa, Peradeniya, SLIIT, NSBM), the institutional brand itself carries weight.

Your Industrial Training Is Work Experience

Industrial training and internships are genuine work experience — treat them that way. Describe them with the same achievement-focused language you'd use for paid roles: what you worked on, what you contributed, what you learned, and any measurable impact you made. 'Developed a customer order tracking module during industrial training at John Keells Holdings that reduced order query response time by 40%' is real, valuable experience.

Include Projects, Activities, and Leadership

University societies, sports captaincy, event organization, academic competitions, community volunteering, and research projects all demonstrate skills. A CV that shows you as an active, engaged individual who leads and contributes outside the classroom is far more compelling than one that lists only your degree.

Your Professional Summary Should Show Ambition and Fit

'Recent Computer Science graduate from the University of Moratuwa, specialising in machine learning and web development. Built 3 full-stack web applications during university including a final year project that achieved 94% accuracy on a sentiment analysis model. Seeking a software engineering role in Sri Lanka's growing tech sector where I can contribute immediately and grow rapidly.'
