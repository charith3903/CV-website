Personal Branding for Fresh Graduates in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

You don't need 10 years of experience to have a strong personal brand. Here's how to build one as a fresh graduate entering Sri Lanka's job market.

Start on LinkedIn Before Graduation

Create and fully optimise your LinkedIn profile before you graduate. Add your degree (even if in progress), your internship experience, projects, and skills. Connect with your lecturers, industrial training supervisors, and classmates. The Sri Lankan employers who will interview you will search for you on LinkedIn — make sure what they find is impressive.

Document Your Projects and Achievements

As a student, you have more achievements than you think: final year project, group presentations, competition wins, society leadership, sports achievements, and volunteer work. Curate and showcase these on LinkedIn, and use them in your CV. They demonstrate skills and character that employers value.

Pick a Niche and Start Creating Content

Even as a student, posting about your field of study builds professional visibility. Share project insights, industry articles you found valuable, your learning from lectures. In Sri Lanka's relatively small professional LinkedIn community, consistent, intelligent posts from a student professional stand out and attract the attention of the right people early.

Show Up at Industry Events

Attend professional association events, career fairs, and industry seminars as a student. The Sri Lankan professionals you meet at CIMA, CIM, CIPM, and IESL events as a student may become your future employers, colleagues, or mentors. Start building these relationships before you need them.
