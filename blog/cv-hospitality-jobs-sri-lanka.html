CV for Hospitality Jobs in Sri Lanka — Hotels and Tourism

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Sri Lanka's hospitality sector — with its luxury Colombo hotels, stunning beach resorts, and growing boutique property market — is one of the country's most exciting employment sectors. Here's how to write a hospitality CV that opens doors.

Focus on Guest Experience Outcomes

The best hospitality CVs in Sri Lanka demonstrate measurable service excellence: 'Achieved TripAdvisor ranking of #2 out of 47 hotels in Colombo', 'Maintained Google rating of 4.8/5 across 2,400+ reviews', 'Grew Food & Beverage revenue by LKR 45M over 18 months through menu innovation and upselling programme.' Guest satisfaction scores and revenue metrics are your most powerful credentials.

International Brand Experience

Experience at international hotel brands (Marriott, IHG, Hilton, Shangri-La, Minor Hotels) carries significant prestige in Sri Lanka's hospitality job market. If you have international experience, place it prominently. Sri Lanka's luxury sector increasingly expects global brand experience for senior roles.

SLITHM and Professional Qualifications

The Sri Lanka Institute of Tourism and Hotel Management (SLITHM) is the country's primary hospitality education institution. Include your SLITHM qualifications, any City and Guilds certifications, and overseas hospitality qualifications prominently. Food safety certifications (HACCP, ServSafe) are valuable for F&B roles.
