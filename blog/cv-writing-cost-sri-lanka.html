CV Writing Cost in Sri Lanka — How Much Should You Pay?

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

CV writing services in Sri Lanka vary enormously in price — from freelancers charging LKR 2,000 to professional services at LKR 15,000+. Here's how to evaluate whether you're getting value for money.

The Sri Lankan CV Writing Market

The Sri Lankan CV writing market broadly breaks down into: budget freelancers (LKR 1,500–4,000) who typically reformat your existing CV with minimal strategic input; mid-range services (LKR 4,000–8,000) who provide proper rewriting with some industry knowledge; and premium professional services (LKR 8,000–20,000+) that deliver strategic repositioning, ATS optimization, and industry-specific expertise.

What Should Be Included

A professional CV writing service in Sri Lanka should include: a consultation to understand your career goals and target roles, a completely rewritten (not reformatted) CV with achievement-focused language, ATS optimization for the specific industry and roles you're targeting, professional design and formatting, a cover letter template, and at least one round of revisions. If a service is not offering all of these, you should question its value regardless of price.

The ROI Calculation

For a mid-career professional in Sri Lanka targeting a role paying LKR 200,000-350,000/month, a professional CV service that costs LKR 10,000–15,000 and results in landing even one job 2 months earlier represents an ROI of 1,300% or more. The question isn't whether you can afford a professional CV — it's whether you can afford not to have one.
