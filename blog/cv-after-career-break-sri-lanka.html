Writing Your CV After a Career Break in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Returning to work after a career break in Sri Lanka requires a specific CV strategy that builds bridges between your past experience and your future ambitions.

Lead with Your Most Recent Relevant Experience

Your professional summary should lead with your strongest professional credential — not your career break. The break is context, not your identity: 'Finance professional with 10 years of experience in Sri Lanka's banking sector. Following a planned 18-month career break for family reasons, I am returning to the workforce with renewed focus and my CIMA qualification completed during this period.' Lead with your value, follow with brief context.

What to Include from Your Career Break Period

If you did anything professionally valuable during your break — freelance work, consulting, volunteering, professional development, community leadership, online courses, or certifications — include it. Even informal professional activities during a career break demonstrate that you stayed current and engaged. Create an entry in your work experience for this period if it's substantive enough.

Address Skills Currency

Sri Lankan employers may worry about skills being outdated after a career break. Pre-empt this by demonstrating currency: mention recent professional development, online courses completed, or industry events attended during your break in your CV or cover letter. Show that you've stayed connected to your profession even while not formally employed.
