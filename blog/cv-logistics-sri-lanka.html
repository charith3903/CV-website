CV for Logistics and Supply Chain Jobs in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

Sri Lanka's logistics sector — anchored by Colombo Port, one of South Asia's busiest transshipment hubs — is a significant employer of skilled professionals. Here's how to write a standout logistics CV.

Certifications and Regulatory Knowledge

Sri Lankan logistics employers value specific certifications and regulatory knowledge: Customs officer licensing, IATA/FIATA qualifications for freight forwarding, Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (CILT) membership, and knowledge of Sri Lanka Customs procedures, import/export documentation, and HS code classification. Include all relevant qualifications prominently.

Quantify Scale and Efficiency Gains

Strong logistics CVs show scale and results: 'Managed daily inventory of LKR 850M across 3 distribution centres', 'Reduced transportation costs by 18% through route optimisation and carrier renegotiation', 'Oversaw clearance of 2,400 TEU monthly through Colombo Port with 98% on-time delivery rate.' Efficiency improvements and cost savings are particularly compelling.

Technology Systems Experience

List your experience with: ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics), warehouse management systems (WMS), transport management systems (TMS), and freight management software used in Sri Lanka's logistics sector. CEVA, DHL, Maersk, and other international logistics companies operating in Sri Lanka have specific system requirements.
