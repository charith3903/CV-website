References on a CV in Sri Lanka — Do You Need Them?

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

The 'References' section on Sri Lankan CVs is widely mishandled. Here's the definitive guide to managing this part of your application professionally.

The Modern Sri Lankan Standard

For most private sector professional roles in Sri Lanka, you do not need to list full reference details on your CV. The standard is to either include a brief line 'References: Available on request' or omit the references section entirely from your CV (the recruiter knows you have them). This saves valuable CV space for content that actually influences hiring decisions.

When to Provide References Upfront

Some Sri Lankan government positions, certain international organizations, and senior executive roles may specifically request references with the initial application. In these cases, provide 2-3 professional references with: full name, job title, organization, phone number, email address, and your relationship to them (e.g., 'Former Direct Manager at [Company]').

Choosing the Right References in Sri Lanka

Professional references should be: former or current direct supervisors (most credible), senior colleagues who can speak to your professional work, clients you've served, or mentors in your field. Personal or character references (family friends, religious leaders) are generally not appropriate for professional job applications in Sri Lanka's corporate sector. Always ask your reference's permission before listing them.

Reference Check Culture in Sri Lanka

Reference checks are standard in Sri Lanka's corporate sector. Most companies will contact 2-3 references before making a final offer, particularly for senior roles. Prepare your references by briefing them on the role and your key achievements to discuss — a well-prepared reference is far more effective than a surprised one.
