Top Job Portals in Sri Lanka — Best Sites to Find Jobs in 2026

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Knowing which job platforms are most active in Sri Lanka can significantly accelerate your job search. Here's the complete breakdown of where to look in 2026.

TopJobs.lk

TopJobs is Sri Lanka's most established and most used job portal, with thousands of active job listings across all industries. Both Sri Lankan companies and international employers advertise here. Set up job alerts, upload your CV, and search by industry, location, and experience level. It's the first place most Sri Lankan job seekers check — and for good reason.

xpat.lk

xpat.lk focuses on professional and management level roles in Sri Lanka, with a strong presence in banking, finance, IT, and senior management. Many multinational companies and leading Sri Lankan conglomerates post here. Particularly good for mid-senior level professional searches.

LinkedIn Jobs

For professional and technical roles, LinkedIn Jobs has become increasingly dominant in Sri Lanka. Roles posted by multinationals, tech companies, and international NGOs are heavily concentrated here. The platform's networking component also gives you direct access to hiring managers.

LankaXpress Jobs

Good for a broad range of vacancies including entry-level, trade, and technical roles across Sri Lanka. Widely used for manufacturing, construction, and operational roles alongside professional positions.

Government Job Portals

For government positions in Sri Lanka, check: The Department of Examinations website, the Public Service Commission of Sri Lanka, and individual Ministry websites for specific vacancy announcements. Government jobs are also advertised in the Ceylon Daily News and other national newspapers.

Direct Company Career Pages

For top Sri Lankan employers — John Keells Holdings, Dialog Axiata, Brandix, MAS Holdings, Aitken Spence — always check their official website career pages. Many senior roles are posted directly on company websites and nowhere else.
