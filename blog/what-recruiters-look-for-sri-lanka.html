What Sri Lankan Recruiters Look for in a CV — Insider Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Understanding how Sri Lankan recruiters actually evaluate CVs gives you a significant advantage. Here's an insider perspective from across the industry.

The First 10 Seconds

Sri Lankan recruiters — like their counterparts globally — spend approximately 7-10 seconds on the initial CV scan. In those 10 seconds they're looking for: job title match, qualification match, career trajectory (ascending or stagnant), current company and industry, and overall professional presentation. If anything in those 10 seconds raises a concern, the CV moves to the 'no' pile before it's read properly.

What Immediately Gets Positive Attention

Recruiters respond positively to: a strong, specific professional headline, clear quantified achievements in the first quarter of the CV, recognized employer brands, prestigious qualifications placed prominently, and professional, clean formatting. These signal that the candidate is worth investing reading time in.

What Triggers Immediate Red Flags

Sri Lankan recruiters flag: unexplained employment gaps without context, very frequent job-hopping (multiple roles under 12 months without explanation), qualifications that don't match the claimed experience level, obvious template CVs with placeholder language, spelling and grammar errors (immediate credibility killer), and inconsistencies between CV and LinkedIn profile.

The Quality of Your Summary

Experienced Sri Lankan recruiters read the professional summary carefully — it tells them whether the candidate understands their own value and can communicate it clearly. A strong summary suggests a self-aware professional who will interview well. A weak or generic summary suggests the opposite.
