CV for Accountants in Sri Lanka — ACCA, CIMA and CA Professionals

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Accounting is one of Sri Lanka's most structured professions — and your CV should reflect the precision and attention to detail that defines great accountants.

Qualification Placement is Critical

For accounting professionals in Sri Lanka, your professional qualification (ACCA, CIMA, CA Sri Lanka) is often more important than your degree. Place qualifications immediately after your professional summary, before your work experience. Include your membership number and status (e.g., 'ACCA Member' or 'CA Sri Lanka — Passed Finalist').

Specify Your Technical Expertise

Sri Lankan accounting employers want specific technical knowledge. Include: specific ERP software (SAP, Oracle, SAGE, Microsoft Dynamics), accounting standards familiarity (SLFRS, IFRS, LKAS), tax knowledge (VAT, income tax, withholding tax), and audit methodology experience (if in public practice). Generic 'accounting software' tells them nothing useful.

Types of Accounting Roles in Sri Lanka

Clearly position yourself for the type of accounting role you want: Public Practice (audit and advisory at BDO, KPMG, EY, Grant Thornton Sri Lanka), Corporate/Commercial (management accounting, financial control, FP&A in corporates), Banking and Financial Services (financial accounting, regulatory reporting, treasury), or Government (Auditor General's department, state-owned enterprises). Each requires a different CV emphasis.

Achievements Over Duties

Even in accounting, where duties can feel standardized, find the achievement story: 'Streamlined month-end close process from 10 days to 4 days through process redesign' or 'Identified tax optimization opportunities saving the group LKR 18M annually.' These distinguish you from the dozens of other accountants with identical duty lists.
