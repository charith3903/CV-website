Top Industries Hiring in Sri Lanka in 2026

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Sri Lanka's economic recovery and diversification are creating growth opportunities across multiple sectors. Here's where the real hiring momentum is in 2026.

Information Technology

Sri Lanka's IT industry employs over 80,000 professionals and targets USD 5B in export revenue. Major hiring companies: 99X Technology, IFS, hSenid Business Solutions, Virtusa, Axiata Digital Labs, WSO2. Average software engineer salary: LKR 150,000–500,000/month depending on experience.

Banking, Finance and Insurance

Sri Lanka has 25+ licensed commercial banks and a growing insurance sector. Key employers: Bank of Ceylon, People's Bank, Commercial Bank, HNB, Seylan Bank, Ceylinco Insurance, Union Assurance. ACCA, CIMA, and banking qualifications command significant premiums.

Manufacturing and Apparel

Sri Lanka's apparel sector (MAS, Brandix, Hirdaramani, Hela Clothing) employs hundreds of thousands and is expanding. Demand for lean manufacturing specialists, quality assurance managers, sustainability coordinators, and production engineers is strong.

Healthcare and Pharmaceuticals

Growing healthcare system, pharmaceutical manufacturing expansion, and medical device sector are driving demand for doctors, nurses, pharmacists, biomedical engineers, and pharmaceutical regulatory professionals. The Apollo Group, Nawaloka, Asiri, and Lanka Hospitals are major employers.

Tourism and Hospitality

Sri Lanka targets 3M+ tourist arrivals by 2026. The country's luxury hotel boom (Shangri-La, Marriott, Cinnamon) and boutique property sector are actively hiring hotel managers, F&B directors, revenue managers, sustainability leads, and hospitality generalists.
