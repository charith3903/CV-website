How to Get Noticed by Recruiters on LinkedIn in Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Sri Lanka's recruitment industry is increasingly LinkedIn-driven. Top recruiters at Potentials, Canixa Group, MAS Holdings, Dialog, and international search firms are using LinkedIn's Recruiter tool to find passive candidates every single day. Here's how to be at the top of their search results.

Optimize for LinkedIn Recruiter Search

LinkedIn Recruiter searches by job title, skills, location, industry, and experience. Every one of these parameters must be correctly filled in your profile. Set your location to 'Colombo, Sri Lanka' (or your actual city). Ensure your current job title matches the roles you're targeting. Add all relevant skills — even obvious ones.

Use Your About Section for Keyword Density

The About section is your primary keyword space. Naturally include the job titles you're targeting, your key skills, and the industries you work in. If you're a 'Project Manager' who could also be called a 'Programme Manager' or 'Delivery Manager', include all relevant terms.

Have a Complete, 100% Profile

LinkedIn shows you 'All-Star' status when your profile is fully complete — and All-Star profiles appear significantly higher in recruiter searches. Complete every section: photo, headline, about, experience (with descriptions), education, skills, certifications, and at least one recommendation.

Get Recommendations

Written recommendations from managers, colleagues, and clients add enormous credibility to your Sri Lankan LinkedIn profile. Recruiters value them as a form of social proof. Ask 3-5 people in your network to write you a recommendation — offer to write one for them in return.

Be Active and Visible

Recruiters also find candidates through post engagement. When you comment on a post from a company or industry leader, everyone who sees that post can see your name, headline, and profile photo — effectively advertising yourself to the Sri Lankan recruiter community.
