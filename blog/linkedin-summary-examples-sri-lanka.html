LinkedIn Summary Examples for Sri Lankan Professionals

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 6 min read

Your LinkedIn summary (About section) is your professional story told in your own voice. Here are examples across key Sri Lankan industries.

Finance Professional Example

'With 10 years of experience in Sri Lanka's banking sector, I specialise in SME credit risk and treasury management. At Commercial Bank, I grew our SME loan portfolio by LKR 2.4B while maintaining NPL below 1%. I'm passionate about supporting Sri Lanka's economic development through responsible financial management. ACCA qualified | IBSL Chartered Banker. Open to senior finance leadership opportunities.'

IT/Tech Professional Example

'Full-stack engineer with 6 years building scalable SaaS platforms for Sri Lanka's growing tech ecosystem. I live for clean code, elegant architecture, and products that genuinely change how people work. Currently at 99X Technology, where I led the rebuild of a B2B platform serving 15,000 daily users. Stack: React, Node.js, AWS, MongoDB. Active on GitHub: github.com/yourname'

Marketing Professional Example

'Brand strategist and digital marketer with 8 years of experience in Sri Lanka's FMCG market. I've launched 12 products across 4 categories, managing budgets up to LKR 250M. My sweet spot is where consumer insight meets digital execution — I've delivered campaigns achieving up to 340% ROI. Chartered Marketer (CIM). Let's connect if you're building a brand in Sri Lanka.'

HR Professional Example

'HR leader with 12 years of experience transforming people strategy at Sri Lanka's largest manufacturing conglomerates. My focus is building high-performance cultures, reducing attrition, and creating workplaces where people do their best work. CIPM Member | SHRM Certified. Currently leading HR transformation at a 3,000-employee manufacturing group. Open to CHRO opportunities.'
