What Is an ATS-Optimized CV? — Complete Guide for Sri Lanka

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are now used by a majority of mid-to-large Sri Lankan companies to filter job applications. If your CV isn't ATS-optimized, it may never be seen by a human. Here's everything you need to know.

What Is an ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System is software that automatically scans, reads, and ranks CVs based on how well they match the job description. Companies use it to manage high volumes of applications efficiently. In Sri Lanka, most corporate companies, multinationals, banks, and large employers now use some form of ATS — including popular systems like Smart Recruiters, Taleo, Workday, and Greenhouse.

How ATS Scans Your CV

ATS systems scan for: keyword matches between your CV and the job description, standard section headings (Work Experience, Education, Skills), correct date formats, and parseable text. CVs in complex formats — with tables, columns, text boxes, headers/footers, images, or unusual fonts — often fail to parse correctly and are automatically rejected.

How to Make Your CV ATS-Friendly

Use a simple, single-column layout. Use standard section headings. Save as PDF (most ATS can now parse PDF) or DOCX. Avoid tables, text boxes, headers, and footers for critical content. Use the exact keywords from the job description naturally throughout your CV. Include both spelled-out terms and acronyms (e.g., 'Applicant Tracking System (ATS)').

The Balance — ATS and Human Reader

An ATS-optimized CV must also be readable and compelling for the human recruiter who reviews it after it passes the ATS. Don't keyword-stuff to the point of making your CV unreadable. The goal is a CV that passes the ATS AND impresses the human — both simultaneously.
