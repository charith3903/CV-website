CV for Sales Professionals in Sri Lanka — How to Write a Winning Sales CV

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 4 min read

If your sales CV doesn't prove your results with numbers, it won't succeed in Sri Lanka's competitive sales job market. Here's how to write one that does.

Numbers Before Everything

Your sales CV must lead with performance data. Open your summary with your most impressive achievement: 'Results-driven sales manager with 9 years of experience in Sri Lanka's FMCG market. Consistently exceeded targets by 25-40% annually. Built and led a 22-person field sales team achieving LKR 2.8B in annual turnover for the South and East regions.' If there are no numbers in your summary, rewrite it.

Achievement Format for Each Role

For every sales role, include: (1) Your target vs. actual performance, (2) Team size if applicable, (3) Key accounts won or grown, (4) New territory or channel developed. Example: '2024: Target LKR 450M → Achieved LKR 612M (136%). Opened 3 new key accounts across the Western Province.'

Show Sales Methodology Knowledge

Sri Lankan sales employers increasingly value structured sales methodology knowledge: SPIN Selling, Challenger Sale, solution selling, and CRM proficiency (Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics). List your CRM experience and any formal sales training in your skills section.
