CV for UAE Jobs from Sri Lanka — Complete 2026 Guide

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 5 min read

The UAE is the most popular overseas destination for Sri Lankan professionals, with over 250,000 Sri Lankans working there. Here's how to maximise your chances of joining them.

UAE CV Format — What's Expected

UAE CVs typically include: professional photo (headshot, business attire), full name and contact details, nationality, visa status/residency, date of birth, marital status, and religion if relevant to the role. While some of this information is optional in Western countries, it is standard in UAE CVs and its absence may seem unusual to UAE employers.

Passport and Visa Status

State your passport and visa status clearly: 'Sri Lankan national, currently based in Colombo. Available to relocate immediately. Valid Sri Lankan passport.' If you have a UAE visit visa or existing UAE residency, mention it — it reduces the employer's visa processing complexity and cost.

Targeting the Right UAE Channels

For UAE job searching from Sri Lanka: LinkedIn (UAE-specific searches), Bayt.com (the Gulf's largest job portal), Naukrigulf.com, Indeed UAE, and the UAE career portals of specific companies. Also consider working with Sri Lanka-based recruitment agencies that specialise in Gulf placements — they often have established relationships with UAE employers.

Key Industries for Sri Lankans in UAE

Engineering (civil, structural, MEP), hospitality (Dubai's massive hotel sector), finance and accounting (ACCA/CIMA-qualified professionals are highly valued), IT, retail management, and healthcare are the sectors with strongest Sri Lankan professional presence and ongoing demand in the UAE.
