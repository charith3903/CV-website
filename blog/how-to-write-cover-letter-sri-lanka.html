How to Write a Cover Letter in Sri Lanka (With Examples)

By The Elegant Grid | Sri Lanka's #1 CV Design Service | May 24, 2026 | 7 min read

Many Sri Lankan job seekers treat cover letters as a formality — a quick paragraph that says "please find my CV attached." But a genuinely well-written cover letter can be the deciding factor in a competitive application, especially for mid-to-senior roles. Here's how to write one that actually makes a difference.

Why Cover Letters Still Matter in Sri Lanka

For competitive positions at top companies in Sri Lanka, a personalised, well-written cover letter demonstrates communication skills, genuine interest in the role, and the initiative to go beyond the minimum. It also allows you to address things your CV cannot — why you're making a career change, why you want to work at this specific company, or how your unique combination of skills fits this specific role.

The Structure of a Great Cover Letter for Sri Lanka

An effective cover letter for Sri Lankan job applications follows this structure: (1) Professional header with your contact details and the date, (2) Recipient's name and designation (never "Dear Sir/Madam" if you can find the name), (3) Opening paragraph — what role you're applying for and your most compelling credential, (4) Middle paragraphs — 2-3 specific achievements and how they relate to this role, (5) Closing paragraph — your enthusiasm for the company, call to action, (6) Professional sign-off.

The Opening Paragraph That Gets Read

Never open with "I am writing to apply for the position of..." — every cover letter does this. Instead, open with something compelling: "After 8 years of building digital marketing programmes for Sri Lanka's leading FMCG brands, I'm excited to bring that growth expertise to [Company Name]'s expanding digital team." Lead with your value, not your intention.

Show You've Researched the Company

The most powerful thing you can do in a Sri Lankan cover letter is demonstrate genuine knowledge of the company. Reference a recent product launch, a CSR initiative, a company milestone, or a strategic direction and explain how your skills contribute to it. "I was particularly impressed by [Company]'s recent move into the digital payments space — having led [Previous Company]'s successful fintech partnership programme, I'm confident I can contribute meaningfully to this initiative."

Keep It to One Page

A cover letter in Sri Lanka should never exceed one A4 page. Four to five concise paragraphs is the ideal length. Sri Lankan hiring managers are busy — your cover letter needs to be impactful and efficient. Every sentence must earn its place on the page.
